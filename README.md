Sum up 19 November's world news in one image: a dense 24-story front page —
**WORLD BRIEFING, Thursday 19 November 2026, 24 stories across 6 continents**,
with a numbered world map, six stat tiles, and every story date-checked.

- **Lead:** the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen.
- **Breadth:** Berlin, The Hague, Gaza, Kuwait, Hormuz, Riyadh, Odisha, Dhaka,
  Yongbyon, Kuala Lumpur, Cepu, Brussels, Hong Kong, Naypyidaw, DR Congo,
  Caracas, Amazon, Canberra, Black Sea, Mogadishu, New York, Goldstone,
  Los Angeles.
- **Dates:** stories reported 19 Nov carry no tag; events from another day are
  labelled WED, TUE, 15 NOV or FRI. No outcome is claimed for the day's
  scheduled events (Shenzhen talks, Berlin meeting, Hague ruling).
- **Program:** `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to the
  output directory. Deterministic: two runs are byte-identical.

Sources: Reuters · AP · Al Jazeera · NASA · IMD · IAEA — as of 14:30 WIB,
19 Nov 2026.
